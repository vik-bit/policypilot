# Northwind Pay — Customer Grievance Redressal Policy

Version 2.4 · Owner: Customer Experience · Fictional sample document for demonstration only.

## 1.1 Scope

This policy explains how customers can raise complaints with Northwind Pay and how those complaints are handled, escalated and resolved.

## 2.1 Complaint Channels

Customers can raise complaints by email or by calling the customer care helpline between 9 am and 6 pm on working days.

## 2.2 Acknowledgement

Every complaint is acknowledged with a reference number within five (5) working days of receipt.

## 3.1 Resolution Timeline

Complaints are resolved within thirty (30) days of receipt. Complex complaints may take longer where the customer is informed of the delay.

## 3.2 Escalation

If a complaint is not resolved within 30 days, the customer may escalate it to the Nodal Officer, whose contact details are available on request.

## 4.1 Failed Transaction Refunds

Where a customer's account is debited but the payment fails, the amount is refunded to the customer within ten (10) working days of the customer raising a complaint.

## 4.2 Compensation

Northwind Pay does not pay compensation for delays in refunds or complaint resolution.

## 5.1 Ombudsman Information

Information about the regulator's Ombudsman scheme is shared with customers upon request.

## 5.2 Complaint Records

Records of all complaints, the actions taken and the final responses are kept for three (3) years.
