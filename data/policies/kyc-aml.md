# Northwind Pay — KYC and Anti-Money Laundering Policy

Version 5.0 · Owner: Compliance · Fictional sample document for demonstration only.

## 1.1 Scope

This policy sets out how Northwind Pay identifies its customers (Know Your Customer, KYC) and prevents its services from being used for money laundering or terrorist financing.

## 2.1 Customer Identification

Every customer must be identified before an account is opened, using a government-issued photo identity document and proof of address. Identity documents are verified against the issuing authority's database where an API is available.

## 2.2 Video-based Customer Identification

Customers may complete identification through a live video call with a trained officer (Video-based Customer Identification Process, V-CIP). Recordings of V-CIP sessions are stored for one (1) year.

## 3.1 Periodic KYC Updation

Customer KYC records are refreshed periodically according to risk: high-risk customers every two (2) years, medium-risk customers every eight (8) years, and low-risk customers every ten (10) years.

## 3.2 Risk Categorisation

Customers are categorised as high, medium or low risk at onboarding, based on occupation, geography, product usage and transaction patterns. Risk categories are reviewed when a material change is detected.

## 4.1 Politically Exposed Persons

Customers are screened against politically exposed person (PEP) lists at onboarding. Accounts of PEPs require approval from senior management before opening.

## 4.2 Sanctions Screening

All customers and beneficiaries are screened against national and United Nations sanctions lists in a daily batch. Matches are escalated to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer the same day.

## 5.1 Suspicious Transaction Reporting

Where a transaction is determined to be suspicious, a suspicious transaction report (STR) is filed with the Financial Intelligence Unit within seven (7) working days of that determination.

## 5.2 Transaction Monitoring

Transactions are monitored against rule-based scenarios such as rapid movement of funds, structuring below reporting thresholds and unusual geographies. Alerts are reviewed by the compliance team within 3 working days.

## 6.1 Record Keeping

KYC records and transaction records are preserved for five (5) years after the business relationship ends or the transaction is completed, whichever is later.
