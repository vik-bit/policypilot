# Northwind Pay — Data Privacy Policy

Version 3.1 · Owner: Data Protection Office · Fictional sample document for demonstration only.

## 1.1 Purpose and Scope

This policy describes how Northwind Pay collects, uses, stores and shares the personal data of customers, merchants and employees. It applies to every product, every employee and every vendor that processes personal data on our behalf.

## 2.1 Lawful Basis and Consent

Personal data is processed only where we have a lawful basis. Customer consent is collected through a single checkbox at sign-up that covers account operation, analytics and marketing communications. Customers who do not tick the checkbox cannot open an account.

## 2.2 Withdrawal of Consent

Customers may withdraw consent by sending an email to the support team. Withdrawal requests are processed within 30 days of receipt. Withdrawal of consent may result in closure of the account.

## 3.1 Data Minimisation

Teams must collect only the personal data needed for a stated purpose. New data fields require approval from the Data Protection Office before they go live.

## 3.2 Data Retention

Customer personal data is retained for ten (10) years after the account is closed. Transaction logs are retained for two (2) years. After the retention period, data is deleted or anonymised in the next quarterly clean-up cycle.

## 3.3 Deletion Requests

Customers may request deletion of their personal data. Deletion requests are completed within 45 days, except where retention is required by law.

## 4.1 Data Storage Location

Personal data is stored with our cloud provider in data centres located in Singapore and the United States. Backups are replicated across both regions for resilience.

## 4.2 Third-Party Sharing

Personal data may be shared with partners such as lenders, insurers and marketing agencies under a written contract. Customers are not individually notified when their data is shared with a new partner.

## 5.1 Personal Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach, the Data Protection Office notifies the regulator within 72 hours of the breach being confirmed. Affected customers are notified only where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to them.

## 5.2 Breach Register

All personal data breaches, including those not reported to the regulator, are recorded in the breach register with the facts, effects and remedial action taken.

## 6.1 Children's Data

Our services are available to users aged 16 and above. We do not collect parental consent for users aged between 16 and 18.

## 6.2 Data Protection Officer

Northwind Pay has appointed a Data Protection Officer whose contact details are published on our website. The Data Protection Officer reports to the Chief Risk Officer.
