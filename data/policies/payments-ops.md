# Northwind Pay — Payments Operations Policy

Version 6.0 · Owner: Head of Payments · Fictional sample document for demonstration only.

## 1.1 Scope

This policy governs wallet accounts, merchant payments, transfers and recurring payments operated by Northwind Pay.

## 2.1 Wallet Limits

A fully verified wallet may hold a maximum balance of 100,000. Outgoing transfers are capped at 50,000 per day.

## 2.2 Merchant Onboarding

Merchants are onboarded after verification of business registration, bank account and beneficial owners. High-risk merchant categories require approval from the risk team.

## 3.1 Fraud Monitoring

Fraud is detected through rule-based checks that run on the previous day's transactions. Fraud rules are reviewed every quarter by the risk team.

## 3.2 Reporting Unauthorised Transactions

Customers can report an unauthorised transaction through the app or the customer care helpline within thirty (30) days of the transaction.

## 3.3 Customer Liability for Unauthorised Transactions

If an unauthorised transaction is reported within seven (7) days, the customer's liability is limited to 10,000. If it is reported after seven (7) days, the customer bears the full loss.

## 4.1 Transaction Alerts

Customers receive an SMS alert for every debit transaction above 500. Alerts for smaller transactions are available in the app's history.

## 4.2 Merchant Settlement

Merchant payments are settled to the merchant's bank account on a T+2 basis.

## 5.1 Recurring Payments

Customers can set up recurring payments (e-mandates) for bills and subscriptions. Recurring debits are processed automatically on the due date without a separate notification.
