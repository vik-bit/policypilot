# RBI Circular — Harmonisation of Turn Around Time (TAT) and customer compensation for failed transactions using authorised Payment Systems (20 September 2019)

Issued by: Reserve Bank of India · RBI/2019-20/67, DPSS.CO.PD No.629/02.01.014/2019-20
Source: https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=11693
Excerpt: requirement text follows the wording of the circular and its Annex. Some passages are shortened and table rows are written out as sentences; the R-numbered headings are added for analysis. Check the source before relying on it.

## R1. Compensation without waiting for a complaint

Wherever financial compensation is involved, the same shall be effected to the customer's account suo moto, without waiting for a complaint or claim from the customer.

## R2. Reversal of failed transfers by T + 1 day

For Immediate Payment System (IMPS) and Unified Payments Interface (UPI) transfers where the account is debited but the beneficiary account is not credited: if unable to credit the beneficiary account, auto reversal (R) by the Beneficiary bank latest on T + 1 day, with compensation of ₹100/- per day if delay is beyond T + 1 day.

## R3. Cash not dispensed at an ATM

For Automated Teller Machines (ATMs) including Micro-ATMs, where the customer's account is debited but cash not dispensed: pro-active reversal (R) of failed transaction within a maximum of T + 5 days, with ₹ 100/- per day of delay beyond T + 5 days, to the credit of the account holder.
