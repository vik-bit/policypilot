# CERT-In Directions under Section 70B(6) of the IT Act, 2000 (28 April 2022)

Issued by: Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In), Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology · No. 20(3)/2022-CERT-In
Source: https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf
Excerpt: requirement text follows the wording of the directions. Some passages are shortened and table rows are written out as sentences; the R-numbered headings are added for analysis. Check the source before relying on it.

## R1. Synchronisation of system clocks

All service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporate and Government organisations shall connect to the Network Time Protocol (NTP) Server of National Informatics Centre (NIC) or National Physical Laboratory (NPL) or with NTP servers traceable to these NTP servers, for synchronisation of all their ICT systems clocks.

## R2. Reporting of cyber incidents within 6 hours

Any service provider, intermediary, data centre, body corporate and Government organisation shall mandatorily report cyber incidents as mentioned in Annexure I to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing such incidents or being brought to notice about such incidents.

## R3. Point of Contact for CERT-In

The service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporate and Government organisations shall designate a Point of Contact to interface with CERT-In. All communications from CERT-In seeking information and providing directions for compliance shall be sent to the said Point of Contact.

## R4. Logs kept for 180 days within India

All service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporate and Government organisations shall mandatorily enable logs of all their ICT systems and maintain them securely for a rolling period of 180 days and the same shall be maintained within the Indian jurisdiction.

## R5. Subscriber records of hosting and VPN providers

Data Centres, Virtual Private Server (VPS) providers, Cloud Service providers and Virtual Private Network Service (VPN Service) providers, shall be required to register accurate information which must be maintained by them for a period of 5 years or longer duration as mandated by the law after any cancellation or withdrawal of the registration, including validated names of subscribers/customers hiring the services, the period of hire and the IPs allotted to them.

## R6. KYC records of virtual asset service providers

The virtual asset service providers, virtual asset exchange providers and custodian wallet providers (as defined by Ministry of Finance from time to time) shall mandatorily maintain all information obtained as part of Know Your Customer (KYC) and records of financial transactions for a period of five years.
