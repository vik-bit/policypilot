# Circular SFR/PAY/2026/19 — Safety of Digital Payments and Customer Protection

Issued by: Sample Financial Regulator (fictional) · Effective: 1 May 2027
Applies to: issuers of prepaid payment instruments and wallets

## R1. Zero liability for prompt reporting

A customer shall bear zero liability for an unauthorised electronic transaction that is reported within three (3) working days of receiving the transaction alert. Beyond seven working days, liability shall be determined as per the entity's board-approved policy, which must be disclosed to customers.

## R2. Alerts for every transaction

Entities shall send an alert by SMS or email for every debit transaction, irrespective of the amount, and every alert shall include a direct option to report the transaction as unauthorised.

## R3. Pre-debit notification for recurring payments

For every recurring debit, the entity shall send the customer a pre-debit notification at least twenty-four (24) hours before the debit, with an option to cancel that instance or the entire mandate.

## R4. Real-time fraud monitoring

Entities shall deploy fraud monitoring systems capable of detecting and blocking suspicious transactions in real time, before the transaction is authorised.
