# Circular SFR/CX/2026/05 — Turnaround Time for Failed Transactions and Complaint Handling

Issued by: Sample Financial Regulator (fictional) · Effective: 1 January 2027
Applies to: banks and authorised payment system operators

## R1. Auto-reversal of failed transactions

Where a customer's account is debited but the transaction fails, the amount shall be reversed automatically by the end of the next calendar day (T+1), without the customer having to lodge a complaint.

## R2. Compensation for delay

If the reversal is not completed within the prescribed turnaround time, the entity shall pay the customer compensation of one hundred (100) per day of delay, credited without the customer having to claim it.

## R3. Acknowledgement of complaints

Every complaint shall be acknowledged within twenty-four (24) hours with a unique reference number, through the channel used by the customer.

## R4. Information about the Ombudsman

Where a complaint is rejected or not resolved within thirty (30) days, the entity shall proactively inform the customer in writing of their right to approach the Ombudsman, with the relevant contact details.
