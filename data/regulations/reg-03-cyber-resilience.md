# Circular SFR/CYB/2026/07 — Cyber Resilience Framework for Payment Operators

Issued by: Sample Financial Regulator (fictional) · Effective: 1 April 2027
Applies to: all regulated payment system operators

## R1. Multi-factor authentication for staff

Multi-factor authentication shall be enforced for every employee and contractor who accesses systems that store or process customer data, not only for administrators.

## R2. Retention of logs

Logs of all information and communication technology systems shall be maintained securely for a rolling period of one hundred and eighty (180) days.

## R3. Reporting of cyber incidents

Cyber security incidents shall be reported to the national cyber security authority and to the Regulator within six (6) hours of being noticed.

## R4. Review of privileged and user access

Access rights of all users shall be reviewed at least every six months, and privileged access rights shall be reviewed every quarter.

## R5. Third-party risk

Critical third-party service providers shall be subject to an independent security audit at least once a year, and the entity shall retain the right to audit them.
