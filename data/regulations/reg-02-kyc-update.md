# Master Direction SFR/KYC/2026/03 — Updates to Know Your Customer Requirements

Issued by: Sample Financial Regulator (fictional) · Effective: 1 March 2027
Applies to: all regulated entities onboarding customers

## R1. Periodicity of KYC refresh

Regulated entities shall carry out periodic updation of customer identification records at least once every two (2) years for high-risk customers, once every five (5) years for medium-risk customers, and once every ten (10) years for low-risk customers.

## R2. Retention of video identification recordings

Recordings of video-based customer identification sessions, together with the audit trail of the session, shall be preserved for at least five (5) years from the date the business relationship ends.

## R3. Continuous screening of politically exposed persons

Screening of customers against lists of politically exposed persons shall be performed not only at onboarding but on an ongoing basis, at least once a month, for the entire customer base.

## R4. Timeline for suspicious transaction reports

A suspicious transaction report shall be furnished to the Financial Intelligence Unit within seven (7) working days of arriving at a conclusion that the transaction is suspicious.
