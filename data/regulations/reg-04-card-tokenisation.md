# Circular SFR/PAY/2026/11 — Card-on-File Tokenisation

Issued by: Sample Financial Regulator (fictional) · Effective: 1 February 2027
Applies to: merchants, payment aggregators and payment gateways

## R1. Prohibition on storing card numbers

No entity in the card transaction chain, other than the card issuer and the card network, shall store the actual card number or other card data. Existing stored card data shall be purged, and repeat payments shall instead use a token issued by the card network.

## R2. Customer consent for tokenisation

A token shall be created only with the explicit consent of the cardholder, obtained through an additional factor of authentication. No charge shall be levied on the customer for tokenisation.

## R3. Additional factor of authentication

All card-not-present transactions and all digital payment transactions above two thousand (2,000) shall be authenticated using an additional factor of authentication beyond the static PIN.
