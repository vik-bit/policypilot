# Circular SFR/2026/014 — Amendments to Personal Data Protection Requirements

Issued by: Sample Financial Regulator (fictional) · Effective: 1 January 2027
Applies to: all regulated payment system operators

## R1. Reporting of personal data breaches

A regulated entity shall report any personal data breach to the Regulator within six (6) hours of becoming aware of it. Affected individuals shall be informed without undue delay, irrespective of the assessed level of risk.

## R2. Storage limitation

Personal data shall not be kept for longer than three (3) years after the end of the customer relationship, unless a longer period is expressly required by law.

## R3. Localisation of payment data

All personal and payment data of customers shall be stored only in data centres located within the country. A copy may be processed abroad for a transaction but must be deleted from foreign systems within one business day.

## R4. Granular and revocable consent

Consent for marketing shall be obtained separately from consent required to provide the service, and shall not be a condition for opening an account. Withdrawing consent shall be as easy as giving it, and shall be possible from within the entity's app or website.

## R5. Data of minors

Before processing the personal data of any individual below eighteen (18) years of age, the entity shall obtain verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian.
